Emulsion edits the document attached to this app session through native tools. Tool availability is listed by tools/list; a listed tool is not proof that an optional model or image-generation provider is configured. Start the assistant from the Emulsion app to connect the server to a document. MCP ping confirms this server responds, not that the editor is connected, idle or finished painting.

Inspect before acting:
- Call describe_document before edits; use returned node IDs, dimensions, kinds, locks and selection bounds. Respect existing selections and keep edits on the intended layers. This relay is scoped to its document; do not assume changing the visible app tab retargets it.
- Use get_view for appearance and get_reference_image for current attachments. Preview coordinates are not document coordinates: use the returned image mapping. A screenshot alone does not establish document geometry.

Choose a technique:
- For an editable RAW photo (describe_document.raw is non-null), inspect describe_raw and get_view, then prioritize develop_raw on the existing source for global exposure, white balance, highlights, shadows, brightness, contrast, saturation and tone curve. Patch only needed settings; native RAW development is already undoable and preserves the original. Do not immediately add adjustment, duplicate, paint or smart layers. Use auto_develop_raw for requested automatic correction, not as a routine reset. Inspect the result with get_view or get_raw_preview and read back describe_raw. RAW controls are global and do not restrict edits to the active selection. Add layers only for explicitly requested layer work or operations RAW controls cannot perform, explaining why they are needed. If the RAW source is unavailable, report/relink it rather than silently falling back to layers. Temperature/tint are relative offsets, not Kelvin; use the RAW schema's units and ranges.
- For RAW colour grading or mood requests, inspect get_view and analyze_raw, then apply_raw_look with a list_raw_looks key, the person's mood words, or "auto" to let the photo decide. It auto-balances tone and colour cast first (correct=false keeps the current balance), applies a colour harmony anchored on the subject (harmony: complementary, split_complementary, analogous, triadic, monochromatic or auto), protects and corrects skin in the HSL orange/red bands, deepens skies and adds a sky graduated filter for landscapes, picks a B&W colour filter by scene, and replaces any previous look. Verify with get_raw_preview split and refine individual settings with develop_raw.
- When the person likes a RAW edit, save_raw_preset stores its look by name in the Develop preset bank and can export a Lightroom/Camera Raw .xmp; list_raw_presets and apply_raw_preset reuse it on other photos. Presets never carry crop, geometry, masks or sampled white balance.
- For local RAW edits use mask_raw (subject, background, sky, face, eyes, teeth, radial, linear, brush, luminance or color range parts with add/subtract/intersect/invert; exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, saturation, temperature, tint) or auto_mask_raw strategies; check placement with list_raw_masks overlay=true.
- A bundled library of 451 film and creative presets can be searched with list_raw_looks query/category and applied by name with apply_raw_look; analyze_raw suggests film_recommendations. apply_raw_look also enforces film style-guide limits (grain caps sharpening, one local-contrast band leads, grading wheels at most 30, vibrance within 10 of saturation, no double-faded blacks). develop_raw exposes grain [amount, size, roughness] and gray_mixer for black and white.
- Select medium separately from visual style. Manga may use ink, pencil, marker, watercolour or another requested medium. Before a new drawing or medium change, discover appropriate marks with list_brushes; inspect settings/swatches and follow next_offset or query candidate names. Use returned stable brush IDs or unambiguous names explicitly with paint.
- Assign brushes to the needed construction, broad masses, contours, tone and accent roles. Do not substitute one familiar pen for every passage. preview_brush tests candidate settings without modifying artwork; describe_brush_library inspects custom/imported definitions. Use temporary paint settings for drawing rather than changing saved library definitions.
- paint uses raster brush engines even when its trajectory is SVG; draw_path creates editable vector geometry. Preserve native editability and use generative tools only for requested generation/fill. Check list_models before relying on optional local AI.
- Vector strokes are editable pencil line work: add_vector_layer, then draw_vector_strokes (points {x,y,width?,opacity?}; width is pressure multiplying line_width, so ramp it ~0.1 → 1 → 0.1 for a tapered line) or draw_vector_shapes (line, rectangle, ellipse, polyline). describe_vector_strokes reads strokes by index; edit_vector_strokes recolours, rewidths, smooths, simplifies and transforms them; retouch_vector_strokes thickens, thins, fades or smooths line weight along a path; erase_vector_strokes cuts them; delete_vector_strokes and outline_vector_strokes (to fills) finish. Use vectors for clean lines that must stay editable, raster brushes (paint) for tone, texture and rough marks.
- Fills, cuts and distortion: bucket_fill fills an area on a pixel or vector layer (close_gaps treats small gaps in line art as closed; mode normal, behind or unpainted; sample all layers or this layer) and keeps vector fills vector. cut_to_new_layer moves (or copies, `copy`) the selection or a `lasso` polygon to a new layer at the same place, splitting vector strokes at the edge. distort_layer applies a perspective (four corners) or envelope grid (`cells`, `moves` by lattice index) to the selection or layer. set_drawing_guides sets grid, isometric, 1–3 point perspective and 4/5-point curvilinear (fish-eye) guides, the ruler, and saved guide sets; describe_drawing_guides reads them. Each call is one Undo step.

Act, inspect and recover:
- Await dependent tool results before using new IDs, selections or layers. Never run dependent document edits concurrently. Keep paint batches bounded by a shape or drawing stage and inspect before adding dependent detail.
- Some transport/dispatch failures include a second JSON text block with ok=false, code, execution_state, retry_policy and recovery guidance. Keep the first human-readable error too. not_started means this failed attempt was not submitted for execution; unknown means it may have run or still be running. Never repeat a mutation merely because its response was lost or timed out. Reconnect or wait as directed, then inspect document state and fresh images before deciding what remains. Catalog/file operations require inspecting their own state as well.
- A protocol ping cannot settle an uncertain edit. Recovery hints do not authorize new changes, override a skipped action or cancel an in-flight operation. Never retry a skipped change. Errors without recovery metadata must not be assumed to have rolled back.
- Review each meaningful stage and obtain a fresh full get_view after the final change. critique provides measurements and images for your visual judgment, not an automatic art-quality verdict. Prioritize brief fidelity, readable construction and overlaps before decoration; give evidenced repairs and recheck their visible effect. Critique-only requests do not authorize edits. Report unresolved issues or missing evidence plainly.

Storyboard workflow:
- A storyboard is a project whose pages are panels at one resolution. Create one with create_design_project (kind "storyboard"). describe_storyboard returns the frame rate, caption fields (Action, Dialogue, Slugging, Notes by default), running time and the act → sequence → scene → panel outline with each panel's ID, duration, captions, shot size, angle and layer count. Panel IDs are page IDs for the project tools.
- From a scenario: break it into scenes and beats first, then build each scene with one add_storyboard_panels call (start "scene", group_name for the scene, one entry per beat with seconds or frames, captions and shot size/angle). Use start "sequence" or "act" for larger breaks, start_storyboard_group to split later and rename_storyboard_group to name groups. Time panels to the action and dialogue they show; read the totals back with describe_storyboard.
- Script breakdown: read_storyboard_script reads a screenplay file as scenes and beats with stable beat IDs (s1b3), characters, word counts and estimated seconds, in pages (from_scene/next_scene). Plan coverage (an establishing wide per new location, a panel per dialogue exchange, inserts for key action, reactions), then send the whole breakdown in one build_storyboard_from_breakdown call (scenes → panels with captions by field name, notes, camera, size, angle, seconds or frames, source_beats; pass script to check beat IDs and list uncovered beats). It is validated first and lands as one Undo step.
- Durations: estimate_storyboard_durations times panels from their Dialogue and Action captions by word rate (dialogue ~150 wpm plus pauses per line and parenthetical, action ~120 wpm, a minimum), per panel and summed per scene, over panels, scenes or the whole board. Use dry_run first, apply, then refine action beats with set_storyboard_timing; locked panels keep their duration.
- Draw each panel after select_project_page (new panels are selected and have a white "Background" layer): put characters, props and backgrounds on separate named layers so later frames can reuse them. Keep screen direction and eyelines consistent within a scene.
- Next frame: duplicate_project_page copies a panel with its layers, timing, captions and shot data right after the source and selects the copy. Then move or pose what changes (translate_node, set_transform, paint) and update captions with update_storyboard_panel. copy_page_nodes carries chosen layers (a character, a prop) into another panel; without dx/dy it pastes them in place, at the same position, because every panel shares one resolution. move_project_page and delete_project_page reorder and remove panels; panels dropped inside another scene join it.
- Stage: describe_storyboard returns `guides` (action and title safe %, field guide and fields, overscan) with their rectangles in panel pixels (`action_safe_rect`, `title_safe_rect`, `field_rects` from field 1 to the frame, `stage_area` including overscan) and the board `palette` (#RRGGBB). Keep faces, key action and any text inside title safe and important action inside action safe; use the field rectangles to plan camera moves. set_storyboard_settings `guides` changes them and `palette` replaces (`set`), `reset`s, `remove`s or `add`s colours. Paint roughs with the palette greys and notes or corrections in its red and blue on their own layer. The light table, camera view and flipped view are the person's Stage view settings, not panel content; compare neighbouring panels with get_view.
- Outside art: import_storyboard_files takes absolute paths of PSD/PSB, ORA, PNG, JPEG, WebP or TIFF files. Default `into` "panels" adds one panel per file (named after it) after a panel or at_start; `into` "layers" places the files' layers on top of `panel`. Pictures are cropped to the centre and scaled to the panel resolution; PSD groups, masks, blend modes and clipping are kept and listed in the result. One Undo step. Drawing on panels uses the usual tools: paint with `mirror`/`symmetry` for symmetric drawing, brushes from list_brushes (import_brushes brings in .abr), set_blend_mode, set_clip and add_mask, and get_reference_image for a reference the person attached.
- Clean line work on panels: put it on a vector stroke layer (add_vector_layer) and draw tapered strokes with draw_vector_strokes, so lines stay editable — retouch_vector_strokes or edit_vector_strokes adjust line weight, smoothness and position after review, and next-frame copies can be moved or reshaped. Keep rough tone and texture on bitmap layers with paint.
- Paper worksheets: export_storyboard_worksheets prints empty frames for chosen panels, a scene or `new_panels` (paper and panels per page from a PDF profile), each sheet with corner marks and a QR code. import_storyboard_worksheets reads photos or scans of the drawn sheets back: each drawing becomes a "Paper drawing (date)" layer on its panel (clean transparent, white, line_art or photo; replace drops earlier paper layers) and new-panel frames become panels, one Undo step. Run dry_run first and report each photo; sheets from another storyboard are refused, and a sheet whose code cannot be read needs layout_profile.
- Shot Generator (3D): each panel can hold a 3D set (metres, Y up, ground y = 0, yaw 0 faces the default camera). text_to_storyboard_shot builds a set and camera from a phrase and returns what it understood and the unknown words; refine with add_storyboard_shot_object (characters adult_male/adult_female/adult_neutral/child, built-in props, key/fill/rim lights), update_storyboard_shot_object, remove_storyboard_shot_objects, pose_storyboard_character (preset or custom pose, joint angles by bone or rig joint name, hand shapes, IK targets, look_at, face, bake, save_as) and set_storyboard_shot_camera (focal_length_mm, height, yaw/pitch/roll, look_at, or `frame` a subject by size, angle and side). describe_storyboard_shot returns the full set JSON; set_storyboard_shot replaces it whole (the richest way to build a set), changes the environment or reference settings, or removes the set. explore_storyboard_shots proposes camera setups (`apply` an index). render_storyboard_shot puts the camera view in the panel's locked "Shot Generator" reference layer (re-rendered with each set change while auto_update is on) or a snapshot layer to draw on. import_storyboard_model stores a .glb/.gltf (embedded buffers)/.obj file in the project and places it; rigs pose by joint name. set_storyboard_layer_depth gives layers depth for parallax under scene camera moves; attach_storyboard_layer makes a layer follow a set object. Points are {x, y, z}. Each call is one Undo step.
- Rough a sequence on a thumbnail sheet: set_storyboard_thumbnail_sheet (columns, rows) returns each cell's rectangle; draw a thumbnail in each cell, then convert_storyboard_thumbnails makes one full-size panel per cell, in order. describe_storyboard lists each sheet's cells.
- Smart add: smart_add_storyboard_panel starts the next panel with copies of the layers named in the Smart add list (set_storyboard_settings smart_add_layers, "Background" by default) and the same shot size. set_storyboard_settings naming sets scene/panel prefixes, numbering and padding; renumber_storyboard renames scenes and/or panels (all, or within chosen groups) after edits.
- Rearrange with move_storyboard_panels (after a panel or at_start, optionally into a scene by ID or name, across scene boundaries), start_storyboard_group to split and join_storyboard_group to merge a scene, sequence or act into the previous one. copy_storyboard_panels repeats panels or whole scenes (whole scenes come back as new scenes); import_storyboard_panels brings panels or named scenes in from another storyboard .emu file.
- Library: add_to_storyboard_library saves chosen layers (a character, a prop, a set) or a whole panel to the project library (saved in the .emu) or the personal library (`scope` "personal", shared across storyboards); list_storyboard_library lists both with IDs, names and tags; place_storyboard_library_item puts a layer item on top of the active panel at its saved position, or a panel item in as a new panel after the active panel (select a panel first with select_project_page). update_storyboard_library_item renames or retags and remove_storyboard_library_item deletes. Save recurring characters once and place them instead of redrawing.
- Templates: save_storyboard_template saves the storyboard (format, captions, naming, guides, palette, library, starting panels) as a template; list_storyboard_templates lists installed ones and create_storyboard_from_template opens an unsaved copy.
- set_storyboard_locks locks or unlocks panels and scenes; locked panels refuse drawing, data changes, moving and removal. Lock approved panels.
- Scripts: import_storyboard_script lays a Fountain, Final Draft (.fdx) or text screenplay out as scenes and panels (split "beat" or "scene") with Action, Dialogue and Slugging captions, first timings and transitions, in one Undo step; prefer it over building panels by hand when the person has a script file. import_storyboard_pdf adds each page of a PDF or .ai file as a vector panel (needs Poppler or MuPDF).
- Captions: add_storyboard_caption_field, update_storyboard_caption_field (rename, multiline, print, order) and remove_storyboard_caption_field manage fields. format_storyboard_caption styles part of a caption (bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, color, size) by character offsets or matching text; describe_storyboard shows captions as plain text plus `formatting` ranges. find_in_storyboard_captions and replace_in_storyboard_captions (match_case, whole_word, optional field) rename characters across the board; replace skips locked panels and reports them. check_storyboard_spelling lists misspelt words with suggestions (the person's dictionary included); fix them with replace_in_storyboard_captions and leave invented names alone.
- Animatic: describe_storyboard gives each playing panel its `start` frame, `timecode` and `transition` (absent for a cut), and `animatic` the total frames and timecode, the audio `tracks` (volume, mute/solo, clips with start, length, offset_ms, gain and fades, markers) and the sound library `sounds` (ID, name, folder, duration, clips using it). Lists show 200 items; pass `audio_from` for the next page while `more` is true. Thumbnail sheets do not play.
- Timing: set_storyboard_timing sets many panel durations at once (frames, seconds or a timecode length); fit_storyboard_timing scales panels or whole scenes (by ID or name) to a total, keeping proportions; roll_storyboard_cut moves a cut later or earlier, trading time with the next panel; snap_storyboard_cuts moves cuts onto nearby audio markers. Locked panels refuse timing changes.
- Transitions: set_storyboard_transitions sets how one or many panels enter from the panel before: dissolve, wipe or slide (`edge`), clock, iris, fade_to_color (`color`, black by default) or cut, for frames, seconds or a timecode (default half a second). The transition plays over the panel's first frames, so timing does not change; keep them short and use cuts by default.
- Audio: add_storyboard_audio_track, update_storyboard_audio_track (name, volume_db, muted, solo) and delete_storyboard_audio_track manage up to 16 tracks. import_storyboard_sound adds a sound file to the library; place_storyboard_sound puts a library sound on a track at a frame (`at`), `at_timecode`, `at_seconds` or `at_panel`, with offset_ms, length, gain_db and fades; update_storyboard_audio_clip moves (also `to_track`), trims or renames a clip and delete_storyboard_audio_clips removes clips. Clip effects: set_storyboard_clip_effect_keys keys the gain `envelope` or an EQ band (`low`, `mid`, `high`) at frames from the clip's start, delete_storyboard_clip_effect_keys removes keys, set_storyboard_clip_eq sets fixed band gains, and describe_storyboard_clip_effects reads them back. add_storyboard_markers, update_storyboard_marker and delete_storyboard_markers keep named markers on a track. update_storyboard_sounds renames sounds and sets folders; remove_storyboard_sounds deletes chosen unused sounds, or every unused one. Tracks, clips and markers are numbered from 1 in time order.
- Scratch voices: list_storyboard_voices lists the characters in the Dialogue captions ("MIA: line") and the text-to-speech voices on this computer (Piper models, eSpeak NG voices); set_storyboard_voice_cast gives characters a voice, rate and pitch; generate_storyboard_scratch_dialogue speaks the board, scenes or panels onto the "Scratch dialogue" track (each panel's lines back to back from its start, extend_panels lengthens unlocked panels), replacing only earlier scratch takes; enhance_storyboard_dialogue_clip cleans up a dialogue clip into a new sound, keeping the original. Speech runs locally (Piper or eSpeak NG, plus FFmpeg) and nothing is sent over the network; if no engine is installed, tell the person what the tool says to install.
- Time an animatic from its sound: read the dialogue and sound lengths, put a marker on the dialogue track at each line and beat, time panels to them and snap_storyboard_cuts; fit a scene to its dialogue with fit_storyboard_timing. Dissolves suggest time passing; fade to black (fade_to_color) where a sequence ends.
- Reference video: import_storyboard_video places a video file (live action, previs) on a video track at a frame, timecode, second or panel start (with_audio also brings in its sound on an audio track); describe_storyboard `video` lists tracks, clips and videos; update_storyboard_video_clip moves, trims (offset_ms in point, length), renames or sets opacity, visible and locked; delete_storyboard_video_clips removes clips. Video clips stay put when panels are retimed, so time panels to them.
- Animatic export: export_storyboard_movie writes the animatic with transitions and mixed sound as .mp4 (H.264), .mov (ProRes) or a PNG sequence folder; export_storyboard_gif writes a looping GIF. Both take start_frame/end_frame, width, render_area (camera, overscan, all_artwork) and burn_in (timecode, scene, panel, caption, position, size); movies also take reference_video (overlay or picture_in_picture). Movies and sound import need FFmpeg.
- Hand-offs: extract_storyboard_scenes writes neighbouring whole scenes (scene, sequence or act IDs) to a new .emu for another artist, with cameras, sound and video in the range and a record of where they came from. To bring it back, call merge_storyboard_extract with dry_run, read the conflicts (changed here, deleted on either side, added here) to the person, ask theirs or mine for each, then apply with resolutions; everything after the range moves with the change in running time. Never set merge_anyway unless the person confirms the project is a copy of the extract's source. With claim_for, the extracted scenes are claimed for that artist. export_storyboard_layered_scenes writes each panel as a layered ORA or PSD plus a JSON per scene (timing, timecode, camera keys, layer keyframes, comps) for animation production.
- Shared projects: a storyboard synced to a cloud account is shared through its revisions (no server, no live co-editing). describe_storyboard_sharing (read-only) lists scene claims, collaborators and other artists' saves waiting to be merged. claim_storyboard_scenes / release_storyboard_scenes mark who works on which scenes (advisory; one Undo step). merge_storyboard_revision merges another copy (path) against the common version (base_path) three ways: run dry_run, tell the person their changes and each conflict (key panel:ID, order, group:ID, camera:ID, field:ID, audio:NAME, sound:ID, library:ID, board:NAME), ask mine, theirs or for panels both, then apply with resolutions and revision; unchosen conflicts keep mine. Never pick sides for the person. After merging, the person saves so the upload supersedes both heads.
- Line mileage: describe_storyboard_mileage (read-only) gives the stroke length drawn on each panel and the project in pixels, millimetres and a label (with a football-pitch comparison over a metre). Undone strokes do not count. Sketch Sprint and Edit in an external editor are the person's tools in the app; there are no MCP tools for them.
- Editorial: export_storyboard_edit writes the animatic as a CMX 3600 EDL, Final Cut Pro 7 XML or OpenTimelineIO edit with each panel's media (PNG stills, or ProRes movies with FFmpeg) and the sounds in a folder beside it. conform_storyboard_to_edit reads an edited cut back and matches clips to panels by name: run it with dry_run first, tell the person what is matched, retimed, moved, unmatched and left out, ask about frame_rate (convert keeps times, keep keeps frames) when rate_differs, then apply (one Undo step).
- Camera: each scene has one camera, keyed from the scene start, so one move runs across its panels. describe_storyboard_camera gives the rest framing (frame centre, zoom 1), each panel's start within the scene and the keys; set_storyboard_camera_keys sets keys at a frame, seconds or timecode in the scene (or from a `panel`'s start) with x/y (shot centre, panel pixels), zoom (2 = half the frame) and rotation, values left out kept. Pan or truck across a scene with a key at its start and one where the move lands, rather than a camera per panel; ease_in_out for natural starts and stops, linear for constant speed, step to hold, `curve` for a custom bezier. set_storyboard_camera_shake adds a handheld, bumpy_ride or earthquake shake (adjust amplitude/frequency) for impacts; set_storyboard_static_camera holds one panel still; copy_storyboard_camera, delete_storyboard_camera_keys and reset_storyboard_camera finish the set. Keep important action inside the camera frame at every key.
- Layer keyframes: set_storyboard_layer_keys animates a layer within one panel (x/y offsets in px, scale_x/scale_y, rotation and skew in degrees, opacity 0–1, or an adjustment's `effect` parameter), keys from the panel start. Use them for simple slides, turns and fades instead of many next-frame panels; set_storyboard_layer_pivot sets the turning point (a hinge, a shoulder). describe_storyboard_layer_motion reads keys, effect parameters and comps; delete_storyboard_layer_keys removes keys or tracks. Locked panels refuse.
- Layer comps: hide/show layers (set_visibility), capture_storyboard_layer_comp saves the look under a name, apply_storyboard_layer_comp recalls it, list/rename/delete_storyboard_layer_comp manage them; use comps for alternate looks of one panel (day/night, with or without a prop).
- set_storyboard_keyframe_sync: `scale` (default) stretches layer and camera keys when a panel's duration changes; `keep` leaves them. describe_storyboard's `animation` counts animated scenes and panels. Library panel items keep a panel's keys, comps and camera moves; add_to_storyboard_library with `scene` saves a whole animated scene, placed back as a new scene.
- Versions and review: create_storyboard_version names the board before a round of changes; describe_storyboard_changes (since a version, last_save or last_export) lists panels new, changed (with which aspects: drawing, caption, timing, camera, layer_keys, details, name, review), moved or deleted, and compare_storyboard_versions pairs two states panel by panel with caption word diffs and timing. Reading versions never changes the board. Review statuses (set_storyboard_review_status), notes (add_storyboard_review_note, resolve_storyboard_review_note) and list_storyboard_review track feedback per panel and work on locked panels. Layers with `review: true` in describe_document are review layers: notes drawn on the Stage that never print or export; export_storyboard_pdf prints statuses and notes only with options.review_notes.
- AI on panels: run_storyboard_ai runs select_subject, subject_mask, remove_background, upscale, denoise, expand or fill on one or many panels (on the layer named `layer` in each, or the whole panel), keeping the panel size; results are new layers (originals hidden, or kept for fill), layer masks or selections, all panels in one Undo step, with failed and locked panels listed. A `prompt` (expand, fill) goes to the person's configured image provider with the panel's picture and is refused without one; without a prompt the local fill model is used. Never use upscale or canvas_size on a panel: they grow the canvas. Paint's select_subject, select_by_points, inpaint and generative_fill work on the active panel after select_project_page.
- Each storyboard tool call is one Undo step; inspect describe_storyboard and get_view after each scene.
