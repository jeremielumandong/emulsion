
Visual-style catalogue — combine with a medium playbook

These are optional design directions, not definitions of entire traditions or promises of expertise. A style is not a brush engine: watercolour can be realistic, abstract or cartoon-like; oil can be flat and graphic. Choose traits from the specific brief/reference. Do not apply every trait in a row or mix all rows together. Any named, unnamed, regional, personal or newly invented style is accepted through the custom workflow below.

For the chosen row, translate the direction into a short working contract: shape/proportion, edge/mark, palette/value, space/composition, and detail level. Preserve explicit user choices over defaults. The review column supplies a style-specific question for the same bounded full-view/detail-view checkpoints; it adds no extra review passes.

Custom and hybrid workflow:
1. Keep the exact requested name or phrase; do not silently replace an unfamiliar style with manga, realism or another familiar label. Extract traits from the brief/reference into the five-part working contract. If the name supplies insufficient information, state a modest interpretation; ask one specific question only if the choice materially changes the result.
2. Select tools from list_brushes swatches and available path/text/layer tools. For an unfamiliar material effect, make a small reversible mark study on a separate layer, inspect it, and use only the effects it actually produces. Do not claim tool availability from an evocative style name.
3. Assign roles when mixing styles: for example, watercolour washes + cubist forms, oil marks + flat comic colour, or charcoal shapes + art-deco symmetry. Resolve conflicts using explicit user constraints; do not average away the defining traits. Limit the trial and subsequent corrections to the studio budget.
4. Carry the exact style phrase in critique.context.style, material technique in medium, and observable obligations in user_constraints. Judge intentional distortion, symmetry, limited values and negative space according to that contract. Full and detail images remain the basis for review; a label alone never proves fidelity.
